# Answer engine content (AEO)

1. Read AGENTS.md and the target record in `content/articles.json`. Identify the reader's concrete question and decision.
2. Preserve the existing article intent and URLs. Make the opening useful on its own: a concise direct answer, relevant scope, dates, and limitations. Use descriptive headings, ordered rankings, comparisons, and FAQs when they help readers.
3. Verify every material number, price, attribution, safety recommendation, and superlative against primary sources. Record source URLs and access dates. If browsing/evidence is unavailable, flag the claim for review; do not invent citations or silently declare it verified.
4. Ranking titles must match the full visible ranking. Explain criteria, weighting, geographic scope, evaluation date, and commercial relationships when actually established. Do not fabricate a methodology, author biography, reviewer, or firsthand experience.
5. FAQs must answer real reader questions and be visible in the initial HTML. Keep JSON-LD answers in sync with the same visible text. A short answer does not replace nuance for safety, finance, or travel decisions.
6. Content carried over from Bolt is unverified sample editorial material. In particular, check incomplete top-N lists, numerical claims, attributed quotes, and author identities before production publication.
7. Validate escaping, semantic heading levels, table-of-contents anchors, related articles, dates, structured data, and no-JavaScript reading. Run the publishing feature tests after changes.
8. Deliver edited content plus a factual verification record and unresolved gaps. Never claim optimization guarantees featured snippets, AI answers, or FAQ rich results.
