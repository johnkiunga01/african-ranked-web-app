# Generative engine discovery (GEO)

1. Read AGENTS.md and the AEO/technical SEO skills as needed. GEO here means generative engine optimization, distinct from the site's geographic editions.
2. Prioritize useful, verifiable public content: clear entities, original research when available, factual sourcing, transparent ranking criteria, accountable bylines, and real update history. Do not manufacture authority or citations.
3. Check that article content and provenance are available in server HTML, with valid canonicals, schema, sitemap, RSS, and real links. Do not add hidden prompt instructions, cloaked text, keyword stuffing, or user-agent-specific claims.
4. `/llms.txt` is an experimental discovery index, not an established ranking requirement, crawler permission, or substitute for robots.txt. Keep it synchronized with canonical articles and communicate its limitations.
5. Changes to crawler access or content reuse policy require the publisher's actual preference. Do not infer permission to block or allow particular AI crawlers, license content, or expose private content.
6. Track evidence with query, engine, date, location, cited URL, and reproducible observations. Separate referrals, citations, and revenue from speculation. Do not invent analytics, search visibility, conversion rates, or ROI.
7. Run publishing tests and validate discovery endpoints after changes. Include unresolved source-verification and production configuration gaps in handoff.
