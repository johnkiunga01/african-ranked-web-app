# Launch Wave 1: 19 posts

Strategy: win one vertical first (safari and wildlife travel, the highest commercial intent), and add one
fact-based anchor post in each other category so the site's "Africa-wide reference" positioning is real from day one.
Edition for all: `global`. Add regional editions only after each post has unique regional content.

Order of work: publish /methodology, /editorial-policy and /monetization-disclosure first. Posts that depend
on official fee schedules or datasets are marked **DATA** and must not publish until a human has opened the
cited source and confirmed each figure.

## Quality bar for every post

- Passes `PublishingGate` with zero failures, then a human opens every cited source before approval.
- Unique angle per post; the similarity guard blocks near-duplicates, so do not template the "best time" posts.
- `markIndexable()` only after review. Start by indexing the 5 strongest posts and the methodology pages, then expand.
