## Editorial review process

1. Verify each assigned CMS article ID, slug and draft revision; **do not create a second article** where an exact or near-duplicate already exists. Confirm original workbook research status and priority order.
2. Read and proofread each manuscript in full, verify specific claims from primary sources, and check dates against the planned publication date. Source access dates represent the 19 November research pass; they are not perpetual verification.
3. Obtain one authentic, relevant and rights-cleared hero photograph per article with recorded creator, licence, caption, credit and informative alt text. Photography **is a blocker**, not an optional decoration.
4. Populate the CMS's dedicated direct-answer, citations, dated-facts, last-verified and methodology/limits fields from each review candidate. Do not paste entire GitHub Markdown into one field while leaving required CMS fields empty.
5. For the food articles, check culinary terminology, ingredient variability, allergen disclaimers and safety claims; for the travel articles, recheck entry, insurance, park charges and advisories immediately before publication.
6. Submit to CMS \`in_review\` only **after** the content, source metadata and hero-image rights requirements are satisfied. Publication remains a separate approval decision.

## Intent and duplication separation

- \`tanzania-safari-guide\`: general destination planning, parks and routes.
- \`african-safari-packages\`: booking-contract and inclusions comparison, not a country guide.
- \`zanzibar-travel-guide\`: general Zanzibar destination choice; avoid copying the dedicated best-time guide.
- \`injera\`: the grain, fermentation, texture and serving method; not a general Ethiopian dishes list.
- \`ethiopian-food-guide\`: dishes and meal choices; link to injera rather than repeating its technical processing.
- \`biltong\`: dried meat explainer and food safety; not a medically unsupported health ranking or unsafe home recipe.

## Scope and limitations

The original imported workbook draft bodies were research **briefs** with explicit placeholders, not publishable copy. These six are newly authored candidate manuscripts informed by the workbook and checked public sources; they have not been pasted into live CMS drafts, and their CMS revision IDs were not available to this chat. The Chrome browser connector currently supports read-only tab inspection, not authenticated writing or submission. The African Ranked Editorial plugin's CMS writing actions were not exposed in this execution.

**Operational hand-off:** A logged-in editor in Work mode or the CMS can open each matching draft, bring the corresponding manuscript into the editor, attach licensed imagery, populate evidence fields and submit the six for review. Preserve all publication status pointers until an editor explicitly approves them.
